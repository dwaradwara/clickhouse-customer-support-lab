INC-007 - Role and Grant Diagnosis

User: support_app
Assigned role: support_reader

SHOW GRANTS FOR support_app:
GRANT support_reader TO support_app

Role assignment from system.role_grants:
user_name: support_app
role_name: NULL
granted_role_name: support_reader
granted_role_is_default: 1

Grant inspection for support_reader:
No matching table privilege was present in system.grants.

Diagnosis:
- support_app had the expected role assigned
- support_reader was active as a default role
- support_reader had no SELECT grant on access_control_lab.customer_events
- the authorization chain therefore stopped at the role

Root cause:
The role existed and was correctly assigned, but the required table-level SELECT privilege had not been granted to that role.

Required minimal repair:
GRANT SELECT ON access_control_lab.customer_events TO support_reader;
