INC-007 - Write Access Denied After SELECT Repair

Purpose:
Verify that the repair did not over-grant the support_app account.

Attempted query:
INSERT INTO access_control_lab.customer_events SELECT toUInt64(4), toUInt32(303), 'test', now('UTC');

Observed result:
Code: 497
ACCESS_DENIED

ClickHouse reported that support_app did not have the required privilege:
INSERT(event_id, tenant_id, event_type, created_at) ON access_control_lab.customer_events

Post-failure row-count check:
SELECT count() AS row_count FROM access_control_lab.customer_events;

Observed row count:
3

Validation:
- SELECT access remained functional
- INSERT remained denied
- the denied INSERT did not modify the table
- the final privilege scope remained read-only
- the repair followed least-privilege access control
